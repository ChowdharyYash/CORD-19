"""
KPI_EXPLANATION.txt - Business Intelligence KPI Logic
=====================================================

This document explains the logic behind each KPI calculated
in the CORD-19 EDA analysis.

=====================================================
1. TEMPORAL KPIs
=====================================================

TOTAL PAPERS
  - Logic: Count of unique cord_uid values
  - Business Use: Dataset size indicator

PEAK YEAR / PEAK MONTH
  - Logic: Year/Month with highest publication count
  - Business Use: Identifies when research activity was highest
  - Expected: 2020-2021 (COVID-19 peak)

YEAR-OVER-YEAR (YoY) GROWTH
  - Logic: (Current Year - Previous Year) / Previous Year * 100
  - Business Use: Measures research momentum
  - Interpretation: 
    * Positive = Growing research interest
    * Negative = Declining (post-pandemic normalization)

MONTHLY PUBLICATION RATE
  - Logic: Papers published per calendar month
  - Business Use: Granular trend analysis
  - Visualization: Line chart shows acceleration/deceleration

=====================================================
2. JOURNAL KPIs  
=====================================================

UNIQUE JOURNALS
  - Logic: Count of distinct journal names
  - Business Use: Measures research diversity

TOP JOURNAL SHARE
  - Logic: Papers from #1 journal / Total papers * 100
  - Business Use: Identifies dominant publication venues

TOP 10 CONCENTRATION
  - Logic: Papers from Top 10 journals / Total * 100
  - Business Use: Market concentration metric
  - Interpretation:
    * High (>50%) = Research concentrated in few journals
    * Low (<20%) = Highly distributed research

JOURNAL DIVERSITY INDEX
  - Logic: Unique Journals / Total Papers
  - Business Use: Alternative diversity measure

=====================================================
3. RESEARCH TREND KPIs
=====================================================

These KPIs track specific research themes over time.
Each uses keyword matching in abstracts.

VACCINE RESEARCH %
  - Keywords: vaccine, vaccination, immunization, mrna, antibody, booster
  - Logic: Papers with keyword / Total * 100
  - Business Use: Track vaccine development focus
  - Expected Trend: Sharp rise in 2020-2021

TREATMENT & THERAPEUTICS %
  - Keywords: treatment, therapy, drug, antiviral, remdesivir, plasma
  - Logic: Same as above
  - Business Use: Track therapeutic research
  - Expected Trend: Early pandemic focus

EPIDEMIOLOGY & TRANSMISSION %
  - Keywords: transmission, spread, R0, quarantine, lockdown, tracing
  - Logic: Same as above  
  - Business Use: Track public health research
  - Expected Trend: Very early pandemic focus

=====================================================
4. HOW TO USE THESE KPIs
=====================================================

FOR RESEARCHERS:
  - Identify trending research areas
  - Find top journals for submission
  - Track field evolution over time

FOR POLICY MAKERS:
  - Monitor research investment outcomes
  - Identify knowledge gaps
  - Track international research contribution

FOR DATA SCIENTISTS:
  - Baseline for NLP/ML projects
  - Training data quality assessment
  - Temporal validation for models

=====================================================
5. LIMITATIONS
=====================================================

- Keyword matching is approximate (not semantic)
- Some papers may match multiple trends
- "No Data" abstracts are excluded from text analysis
- Journal names may have slight variations
- Date parsing handles known formats only
